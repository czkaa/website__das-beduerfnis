Description:

Photographic exhibition + presentation of a photobook. 4 month in Southeastasia. Searching for aesthetic phenomena. Where do I begin? Aesthetics. Unaesthetics. Globalized aestheticization. Plastic chairs, brooms, colours, shapes and everywhere portraits of the King. I am trying to create something open, experimental, constellative – looking for different forms and their relation to each other. What comes out is an unfinished story of seeking things, finding kings and hiding things.
